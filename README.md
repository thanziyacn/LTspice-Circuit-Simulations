# LTspice Circuit Design & Simulation ⚡

**Exploring Analog Electronics Through Circuit Design, Simulation and Analysis**

## About the Project

This repository documents my practical learning journey in analog electronics and circuit simulation using **LTspice by Analog Devices**.

As an Electrical and Electronics Engineering student, I am exploring the relationship between theoretical circuit concepts, hardware laboratory experiments and simulation-based analysis.

The objective is to develop a strong understanding of electronic circuits through systematic design, simulation, waveform interpretation and technical documentation.

- Develop practical skills in electronic circuit design and simulation.
- Understand the electrical characteristics of semiconductor devices.
- Analyse voltage, current and frequency responses.
- Apply circuit theory to simulation-based experiments.
- Compare theoretical calculations with simulated results.
- Build a structured portfolio of analog electronics experiments.

## Experiment Documentation Methodology

Each experiment follows a structured engineering workflow:

1. **Problem Definition:** Identify the circuit and its objective.
2. **Theoretical Analysis:** Understand circuit operation and perform mathematical calculations.
3. **Circuit Design:** Develop the schematic using LTspice.
4. **Simulation:** Select the appropriate analysis method.
5. **Waveform Analysis:** Examine voltage and current responses.
6. **Result Validation:** Compare theoretical and simulated results.
7. **Documentation:** Record observations, results and conclusions.
